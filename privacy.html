Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Curious Goat Publisher is a private tool used only by the Curious Goat team to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What we collect

When our team account signs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the tool an access token, a refresh token and our account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar). The tool uses them only to upload our videos to our account and to check their upload status.

How it is stored

The tokens are stored only on our own computer, never on a server, and never shared, sold or used for advertising. We do not collect information about TikTok viewers or any other users.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. We then delete the stored tokens.
